📊 Understanding Balance Metrics

What is "Balance"?

Balance measures how evenly students are distributed across classes. Perfect balance means each class has similar numbers of students in each category.

Balance Quality Ratings

  • Perfect: Nearly identical distribution across all classes
  • Excellent: Very minor differences between classes
  • Good: Some variation but still well-balanced
  • Fair: Noticeable differences between classes

What is "Variance"?

Variance is a statistical measure of how spread out the numbers are. Lower variance = better balance.

  • 0.0-0.5: Perfect balance (all classes nearly identical)
  • 0.5-1.0: Excellent balance (minor differences)
  • 1.0-1.5: Good balance (acceptable variation)
  • 1.5+: Fair balance (noticeable differences)

Example

If you have 5 classes and 45 girls total:

  • Perfect: Each class has exactly 9 girls (variance = 0.0)
  • Excellent: Classes have 8, 9, 9, 9, 10 girls (variance = 0.4)
  • Good: Classes have 7, 9, 9, 10, 10 girls (variance = 1.2)
  • Fair: Classes have 6, 8, 9, 10, 12 girls (variance = 2.0)

Friend Satisfaction

Percentage of students who have at least one friend from their friend list in their assigned class. Higher is better!
